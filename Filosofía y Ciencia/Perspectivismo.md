Giere, R. N. 2006 p 13 Scientific Perspectivism
perspectivism forces us to say that “the nucleus has no nature in itself and we can only answer questions about it once a particular perspective is specified” (2015, 160). Giere seems to endorse this conclusion and submits that this is what allows the position to avoid “silly relativism” and salvage realism in the face of different perspectives (2006, 13)

Me gusta esta mirada: realismo para el científico, perspectivismo para el filósofo.
