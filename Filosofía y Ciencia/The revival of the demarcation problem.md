The demarcation problem should not be relived, it just has to be discovered as a problem that has never disappear even in the most contemporary writings. Isn´t it guiding the interests and grounding the inquiries values each time a debate appears about anything connected with science in philosophical terms? Just that it is not implicitly conceived as a general problem any more, but it has been granulated. In any philosophical discussion the assertion remains so obvious that nobody cares about expressing it: this is science, not that. Now, what happens when an issue is so comprehensive that all the practices and institutional doman of what we use to call science is compromised in its results? Those problems are grouped in the so labeled general philosophy of science. And the demarcation problem perhaps should be removed from the demonization suffered in the last decades at least in those cases, just to be fear.
