This characterisation, I argue, may leave her own account contaminated by some of the metaphysical assumptions underlying the view she criticises, particularly through its continuing reliance on the category of the abstract to explain modelling and representation. Indeed, Carrillo and Knuuttila (2023) reject abstraction understood as omission of mechanistic details, rather than the category of abstract models itself. My concern centres on whether the attribution or denial of a target depends on its classification as abstract or concrete, actual or merely possible.
