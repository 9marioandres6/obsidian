### 🧭 Step 1 — Find a host supervisor

You look for a researcher whose work clearly connects with your topic — in your case, _robustness analysis, philosophy of science, methodology, epistemology of models_, etc.  
That person will act as your **temporary academic mentor** during your stay.

They will:

- Write an **invitation letter** or confirm they agree to host you.
    
- Help you access the department, seminars, and library.
    
- Often sign your funding paperwork (for DAAD, Fulbright, COST, etc.).
    

They **do not** replace your main PhD director in Argentina — they’re just your host for the stay.

---

### 📄 Step 2 — You and your host agree on a short plan

You’ll send them:

- A 1-page description of what you’ll do during the stay (e.g. “work on the epistemic implications of robustness in model-based science, focusing on Stegenga’s framework”).
    
- Proposed dates (e.g. April–July 2026).
    
- How the stay contributes to your dissertation.
    

If they accept, they’ll issue a **letter of support or invitation**, which is what you’ll attach to your funding application.
